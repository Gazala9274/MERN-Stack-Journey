Lunar Eclipse

A lunar eclipse occurs during a full moon when the Earth moves directly between the Sun and the Moon, casting its shadow onto the lunar surface. As the Moon travels through Earth's inner shadow (the umbra), it can turn a deep rusty-red or orange color because the Earth's atmosphere filters and bends sunlight toward it. Unlike a solar eclipse, a lunar eclipse is completely safe to watch with the naked eye from anywhere on the night side of the Earth.
